Getting started

1. Keep the Discord desktop client (or Vesktop with arRPC) signed in on the Orca host, or enable the companion bridge.

2. Approve the plugin's permissions when Orca asks.

3. Open this tab from the right sidebar.

4. Click Show Status to confirm what is being transmitted.

Troubleshooting

Vesktop Flatpak: enable Rich Presence via arRPC. The IPC socket lives under .flatpak/dev.vencord.Vesktop/xdg-run.

Competing RPC: another IDE or overlay can overwrite activity. Click Reload RPC or wait for the 90s heartbeat.

Logs: ~/.local/state/jondmarien-discord-presence/plugin.log (Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\jondmarien-discord-presence\plugin.log)

Settings: Orca Settings → Plugins. There is no deep link from this panel.
